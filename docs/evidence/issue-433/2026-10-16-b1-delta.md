## Native initialization and cleanup

HTTP health and theme availability precede the pinned entrypoint's JavaScript-cache generation and docservice/converter restart. Admitting an editor at that intermediate point [produced connection loss and force-save error 1](native-initialization-failure.json.gz). Qualification waits for the positive PID-1-owned final log-tail phase, then checks health again, within the existing readiness budget. It does not replace readiness with a fixed sleep or suppress the connection error.

Force-and-close campaign `b1-amd-0a7f88f01e344f00a4d4d0cee40bef47` completed with stable docservice/converter PIDs. Independent unforced-close campaign `b1-amd-0f5d3ed8bd8242dfa6e8346bb5cc322a` also completed. Each campaign preserved Docker resource identities/states, including original and unrelated images, and left no live owned process groups or new native-browser processes.

These B1 campaigns do not by themselves certify the complete broker workflow, independent-reader comparison, release export/import, WAN-free restart, acceptance machine or LAN deployment. Those claims require their own evidence.

## Complete local workflow

[Runtime observations](runtime-observations.json.gz) bind the complete default `make verify-office` run to its verifier-source fingerprints and qualified image. Actual native DOCX/XLSX/PPTX edits were published and read back in fresh sessions. The independent content judge and LibreOffice reopening passed; page counts were 1→1, 3→3 and 3→3. Recorded font substitution and page counts are observations, not a fidelity pass/fail judgement. The [input and published documents](workflow-sample-exports.zip) retain the tested bytes.

The full run left no owned process groups or new native-browser processes and preserved the Docker inventory. PPTX content corruption and browser interruption controls failed as expected with the same cleanup guarantees. Native diagnostics attributed 14 expected unload-policy denials; unexpected console errors and page errors were zero. [Local checks](local-checks.json.gz) retain the owning test and lint receipts; [shared-surface checks](shared-surface-checks.json.gz) cover the real WebUI gateway and deterministic workspace UI, not LAN acceptance.

An additional initialization-interruption attempt failed before signal injection while 9 images and 14 volumes disappeared from the Docker inventory. Attribution is unresolved; that attempt is not passing interruption evidence. [Recovery evidence](initialization-recovery.json.gz) retains the failure, explicit restoration of only the original and qualified Office images, and the subsequent successful SIGTERM control. Restoration preserved the then-current unrelated resources; it did not reconstruct unrelated removed images or volumes. The repeated control retired all 34 owned process groups, left no new native-browser process, and preserved its Docker inventory. The verifier itself did not pull or rebuild an image.
